Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

ESpeak ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

ESpeak is designed to work entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information.

  • No account or registration required.
  • No name, email address, or contact information collected.
  • No location data collected.
  • No usage analytics or tracking.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

All app data — including saved words, saved phrases, drill progress, and streak count — is stored locally on your device using SQLite. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

3. AI Content Generation (Groq API)

ESpeak uses the Groq API to automatically generate new vocabulary words, sentence drills, and professional phrases. When this feature runs:

  • Only generic prompts are sent to the Groq API (e.g., "generate a professional English word").
  • No personal information, device identifiers, or user-generated content is included in these requests.
  • Groq's own privacy policy applies to their service: groq.com/privacy-policy.

4. Push Notifications

ESpeak may request permission to send local push notifications for the daily Word of the Day reminder. These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device — no notification servers or third-party push services are used. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

5. Third-Party Services

ESpeak does not integrate with advertising networks, social media platforms, or any analytics services. The only external network request the app makes is to the Groq API for content generation, as described in Section 3.

6. Children's Privacy

ESpeak does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The app does not collect personal information from any user.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact
